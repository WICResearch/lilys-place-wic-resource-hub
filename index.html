Could WIC help your family?

WIC is more than food benefits.

WIC can provide nutritious foods, nutrition education, breastfeeding support and connections to other health and community services.

1

Could you qualify for WIC?

Use the WV WIC Eligibility Screener to answer a few quick questions and see if you may qualify for WIC benefits.

Check WIC Eligibility →

Scan the QR code with your phone or select the button above. The screening tool provides an estimate of potential eligibility. Final WIC eligibility is determined by the WIC program.

2

Ready to apply?

Use the official WIC participant application. On a phone, use the button; on another device, you can scan the QR code.

How to read these numbers

  • Huntington ZIP statistics come directly from the uploaded WV Map Project Data.xlsx source rows for 25701–25705.
  • The Cabell County active-family count comes from the uploaded County Data.xlsx.
  • Valley East/West mobile enrollment metrics come from the uploaded FY2026 outreach workbook.
  • Estimated eligibility and participation measures are planning estimates, not individual eligibility determinations.
  • No participant-level data should be placed in this GitHub repository.

About this hub

Built to make the next step easier.

This resource hub is designed for Lily's Place families and Huntington-area residents. It brings local resources, WIC access and transportation information into one place.

Organization hours, services and eligibility requirements can change. Confirm details directly with the organization before traveling.
